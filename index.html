Shared Classroom Device

One tablet per class — every teacher, zero password juggling

Many centers keep a classroom iPad at the check-in desk. Owners enable shared mode once — location admins with device permission can help tune session timeouts. Teachers switch with a quick PIN and the tablet locks when idle.

  • Owner-controlled setup: Choose individual devices or shared classroom mode per center, with configurable teacher session and tablet lock timeouts.
  • Delegated device settings: Grant trusted admins permission to adjust shared-tablet timeouts without handing over full owner access.
  • PIN teacher switching: After the first sign-in, teachers hand off the tablet with a 4-digit PIN — no re-entering email and password between shifts.
  • Secure PIN reset: Owners and admins generate a new teacher device PIN by email — the PIN is never shown in the app for security.
  • Clock in/out on shared tablets: Staff clock in before logging activities; owners review time logs and outlier sessions from the admin dashboard.
  • Diary & record stay visible: Class diary notes and activity history remain browsable on the shared device — only logging and clock actions require the active teacher.

For Classroom Teachers

Log updates in 3 taps, then get back to the kids

Teachers want to teach, not type. Log activities from a class roster, add diary notes, browse class history, generate end-of-day reports, and keep parents in the loop — on a personal phone or the classroom tablet.

  • 3-Tap Activity Logging: Log meals, naps, bathroom breaks, photos, moods, and notes in seconds — for one child or in bulk.
  • Check-In & Check-Out: Record child arrivals and pickups through configurable activity categories, including who picked up.
  • Class Diary: Add timestamped diary notes tagged to individual students — visible to admins and browsable on shared devices.
  • Class Record: Browse class activity and daily report history with date-range filters, category filters, and collapsible date sections.
  • Clock In & Out: Teachers clock in before logging activities; works on personal devices and shared classroom tablets.
  • Shared Tablet PIN Flow: Switch teachers with a PIN, auto-lock when idle, and continue browsing diary and record between sessions.
  • Daily Reports: Generate summarized end-of-day reports for individual students or your whole class.
  • Quick Sign-In: Invitation-based teacher signup, remember-me, and Face ID / fingerprint unlock on supported devices.
  • Class Roster at a Glance: See allergies, parent notes, and emergency contacts right on each student's card.
