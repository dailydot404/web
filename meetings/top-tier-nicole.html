Admin app

For you as owner — structure the centre, keep licensing-ready records, and see the day across locations without living in the app.

Admin · day-to-day

Run both Top Tier sites from one place.

  • ✓Locations & classes — Brookswood multi-age and school-age under one centre account.
  • ✓Student & teacher rosters — assign to classes; parent and emergency contacts on file.
  • ✓Invitations — invite teachers and parents by secure code (share by text if you want).
  • ✓Bulletins — your announcements to parents: a class, a location, or the whole centre; attach photos or PDFs.
  • ✓Staff hours — review clock-in/out; download timesheet PDFs when you need them.

Admin · records centres need

Licensing packs, documents, and the student activity log.

  • ✓Activity history — every meal, nap, check-in, photo, and note in one timeline; daily-report PDFs.
  • ✓Document vault — PIN-protected immunizations, consents, CRC, first aid, ECE certificates; expiry reminders.
  • ✓Licensing visit export — attendance register, staff hours, emergency contacts, and related summaries in one pack.
  • ✓Safety logbook — fire drills, lockdowns, inspections, incidents — per location, with PDF.
  • ✓Policies & waitlist — staff acknowledge current policies; hold interest per class until a spot opens.

We support your records and visit packs — we don’t replace your facility licence.

Teacher app

For your ECEs on the floor — log the day in a few taps, keep the activity record accurate, and hand off cleanly between shifts or sites.

Teacher · classroom day

Roster first — so logging stays with the kids.

  • ✓Class roster — allergies, parent notes, and emergency “Urgent” on each child’s card.
  • ✓Activity log — meals, naps, bathroom, moods, photos, notes — one child or bulk.
  • ✓Check-in / check-out — arrivals and pickups on the day’s log, including who picked up.
  • ✓Daily reports — today’s summary for a child or the class; history in Record.
  • ✓Class Diary & shared tablet — short shift handoffs; PIN switch between teachers — not chat clutter.

Teacher · hours & credentials

Clock time and keep certificates current.

  • ✓Staff clock-in / out — clock in before logging; download your own timesheet PDF.
  • ✓Same-day class moves — float to another room or location for today; return home at end of day.
  • ✓Certificate vault — upload CRC, first aid, ECE as PDF or photo, with expiry dates.
  • ✓Policy acknowledgments — read centre policies and confirm current versions.

Certificate uploads and policy acknowledgments live in the same vault and policy tools as Admin.

Parent app

For Top Tier families — a clear view of the day, photos, and centre news, without reconstructing everything at pickup.

Parent · staying in the loop

Live updates from the classroom activity log.

  • ✓Activity feed — meals, naps, photos, moods, and notes as teachers log them.
  • ✓Daily reports — end-of-day summaries by category, saved in the app.
  • ✓Photos — view and download classroom photos; update the child’s profile photo.
  • ✓Bulletins — your announcements, with attachments; read/unread tracking.
  • ✓Child details — allergies, emergency contact, and notes parents can keep current.

No in-app parent↔teacher chat. Day updates are activities and reports; centre news is bulletins from Admin.

Between your sites

Teachers and children can move for the day — without rebuilding the roster.

Multi-age and school-age stay separate on the roster. When a teacher covers the other site — or a child spends part of the day there — same-day moves keep the record accurate.

Teacher move

Same-day float

Home class stays. For today they see the other room’s kids and log there. End of day they return home — permanent reassign only when you choose it.

Student move

Same-day for a child

Move a child to the other site for the day — attendance and activities follow. Parents still only see their own child. Next day they’re home unless you make it lasting.

Try both in Top Tier Demo. Optional facility-wide access for trusted staff who should see every classroom.
