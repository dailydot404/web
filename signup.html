What happens next

The form emails our team. We reply. Then we create your centre in the Admin app.

1. We contact you

Expect an email from us, not an automatic app account. We will walk through whether DailyDotKids fits how you run today.

2. We set you up

We create your daycare in the Admin app and help you add locations, classes, teachers, and children.

3. You invite your people

Teachers and parents join with an invitation from you — not this website form. Optional: one shared tablet in the classroom.

Common questions

About registering

Is this only for British Columbia?

No. Licensed centres across Canada can register. Pick your province or territory on the form. Licensing rules stay local — see Compliance.

Do I need to contact a developer network?

No. Use this page. The form emails us and we get back to you.

Is the pilot free?

Yes — for a limited number of centres. There is no setup fee. If we later offer a paid plan, we will talk with you before anything is billed.

I downloaded the app. Why can’t I sign in?

The App Store and Google Play listings are for centres we have already set up. Register here first. After we create your centre, you invite staff and families from the Admin app.
