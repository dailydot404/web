Security & data residency

Canadian daycare data stays in Canada. We design DailyDotKids so children’s and family records are hosted in the same country (and cloud region) as the centres we serve — not shipped to a distant default region.

Last updated September 2026

In one sentence

For Canadian centres, production daycare records are kept in Canada on Google Cloud, isolated per centre, encrypted in transit and (for sensitive fields) at rest, and accessible only to people your centre invites — we do not sell personal information.

Where production data lives

  • Canadian daycares: Centre database records, classroom photos, and vault files are hosted in Google Cloud northamerica-northeast1 (Montréal, Québec) — in Canada, with the company based in Langley, British Columbia.
  • Local to the country we serve: Our approach is to keep operational daycare data in-country for the market we operate in. Today that means Canada for Canadian centres. If we expand to other countries later, the same principle applies: keep that country’s daycare data local to that country/region where we offer production hosting.
  • Workloads in that region: App Engine (API), Cloud SQL (database), Cloud Storage (photos and vault files), and Secret Manager (runtime secrets).

Push notifications and some email delivery may use global networks so a message can reach a phone or inbox. That is for delivery only — centre records in our database and file storage for Canadian daycares remain in Canada. See Subprocessors.

Who can access centre data

  • Scoped by daycare: Admins, teachers, and parents only see data for centres they are invited to.
  • Role-based: Owners/admins manage roster and settings; teachers run the classroom day; parents see their own children.
  • Anthor staff: Production access is limited to people who need it to run or support the service, under least privilege.

How we protect data

  • HTTPS for app and API traffic.
  • Encryption at rest for sensitive fields (for example allergies, dates of birth, emergency contacts, activity notes, vault document metadata) and encrypted vault files — see our Privacy Policy.
  • Hashed credentials: passwords and PINs are one-way hashed; we cannot read them back.
  • Secrets: runtime credentials are stored in Google Secret Manager, not in public source control.
  • Managed infrastructure: Google Cloud operates the underlying physical security, patching, and regional controls for the services we use.

What we do not claim

  • We do not claim SOC 2, ISO 27001, or HIPAA certification at this time.
  • We do not replace your duties as a licensed child care operator — see Compliance.
  • No system is perfect. We focus on practical controls, honest documentation, and clear contact paths when something goes wrong.
